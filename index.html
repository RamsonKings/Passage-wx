One per line: name; lat; lon; climatological along-track current for the leg ending here (+ fair / − foul). Lat S and lon W negative.
    Times are ship LT. Leg STW = ordered speed less weather loss; Leg SOG = Leg STW + current, mean on the leg ending at that waypoint; violet ✱ = no forecast current for most of that leg, so the route's climatological current is used. Rel = where wind / swell comes FROM relative to ship's head. Current set = direction TOWARDS. Along = current component on the course (+ fair, − foul). ECMWF IFS wind and ECMWF WAM waves are selected separately. ECMWF WAM 9 km is the default; missing wave partitions are supplemented from GFS-Wave when available and labelled in the report. Marine/current range varies by model and location. “–” = missing/out of range or too near land. Not a substitute for official warnings (NAVTEX / SafetyNET, JTWC / JMA).

    Basis. Storm-centre 300 nm: Tomini Navigation Manual Rev03 Sec 13 (keep ≥ 300 nm from storm centres, otherwise inform the office). Roll checks follow the principles of IMO MSC.1/Circ.1228: synchronous rolling when encounter period ≈ roll period, parametric rolling when encounter period ≈ half the roll period (head or following seas), successive high waves when wavelength is 0.8–2.0 L and wave height exceeds 0.04 L. All other values are Master's settings, not company limits. Roll period by the IMO Intact Stability Code formula T = 2·C·B / √GM, with C = 0.373 + 0.023·(B/d) − 0.043·(L/100). Encounter period Te = 3T² / (3T + V·cos α), V in knots, α = wave direction relative to the bow. The tool flags risk conditions; it cannot predict roll angles.
